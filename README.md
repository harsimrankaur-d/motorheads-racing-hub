# Motorheads Racing Hub

Design a modern, high-performance website for "MOTORHEADS", the official student motorsport and engineering team of BMS Institute of Technology and Management (BMSIT&M), Bengaluru. The site should feel like a hybrid of the official Formula 1 (formula1.com) and Ferrari websites — dark, cinematic, high-contrast, and built for speed.

Visual Direction (F1 x Ferrari aesthetic):

Dominant color palette: deep black/charcoal (
#1a1a1a–
#2b2b2b) backgrounds with a bold racing red (
#d31411/
#ff1e1e) as the primary accent, plus crisp white for contrast — mirror Ferrari's red-on-black drama and F1's dark, data-dense UI.
Bold, italicized, condensed sans-serif headings (like F1's own display font) — aggressive, slanted, all-caps for section titles ("OUR MISSION", "WHY SPONSOR US").
Diagonal/angled section dividers and speed-line graphics, chevron/arrow motifs (echoing the Motorheads lightning-bolt logo), and checkered-flag patterns as footer/border accents.
Full-bleed hero image/video of the go-kart or team at the track, with a dark gradient overlay so white/red text pops.
Card-based layout with subtle motion (hover lifts, animated underlines) for divisions, tiers, and timeline — similar to F1's news/standings cards.
Generous whitespace between sections but a "dense, data-rich" feel in stat blocks (like F1 standings tables).

Site Structure & Content (from official brochure):

Hero Section — Motorheads logo, tagline "SPONSORSHIP BROCHURE" energy but reworked as a homepage hero (e.g. "MOTORHEADS — Built to Race. Engineered to Win."), CTA buttons ("Partner With Us" / "Meet the Team"), contact email teammotorheads@bmsit.in.
About / Embrace Technology — Team Motorheads is BMSIT&M's official student motorsport & engineering team: 25 undergraduates who design, analyse, fabricate, and race their own combustion (CV) and electric (EV) go-karts at the Indian Karting Race (IKR). Include the pull-quote: "We are not a club that meets on weekends... Every deadline is a race." Include team group photo.
Our Mission — 4 pillars with icons: (1) Carry BMSIT&M's flag onto the national motorsport stage, (2) Push sustainable mobility through competitive student-built EV tech, (3) Forge technically sharp, leadership-ready engineers under real competition pressure, (4) Give members unfiltered industry exposure before their first job.
Our Divisions — 6 cards with icons/images: Chassis, Powertrain, Electrical, Vehicle Dynamics, CAD & Analysis, Marketing & Finance — each with the short description from the brochure.
Competition Participation & Achievements — GKDC 2026 (Kari Motor Speedway, Coimbatore, Feb 2026) and EKVC 2026 (Hindustan College of Engineering & Kari Motor Speedway, March 2026 — won Best Cost Award). Include "Our Journey" tagline block.
Why Sponsor Us — 4 benefit blocks with icons: Your Logo Moves, Talent Before Everyone Else, EV Is Now Be In It, Reach That Actually Lands. Plus an "Our Impact" stat strip: Best Cost Award EKVC 2026 | 3 National Competitions | 25+ Active Team Members | Growing Digital Community.
Sponsorship Tiers — Present as tiered pricing/benefit cards (like F1 partner tiers): Pole Position Partner, Factory Team Affiliate, Technical Tier, Pit Crew Contributor, Performance Partner — with their respective benefit bullet points.
Footprints of Excellence (Timeline) — Vertical/road-style timeline (as in the brochure) from IKR 2019 (Best Acceleration) → IKR 2020 (Future Stars) → IKR 2023 → SEVC 2025 Chennai (Best Design) → GKDC 2026 → EKVC 2026 (Best Cost Award), each with location and photo.

Official channels: teammotorheads@bmsit.in, Instagram @motor.heads_bmsit, LinkedIn "Motor Heads - BMSIT&M".
